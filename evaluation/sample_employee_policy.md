# Sample Employee Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave during each calendar year. Employees may carry over no more than 5 unused annual-leave days into the following year. Any additional unused days expire at year end unless local law requires otherwise.

## Remote work

Employees approved for remote work may claim a one-time equipment allowance of USD 500 for a desk, chair, monitor, or other approved equipment. Receipts are required for reimbursement.

## Health benefits

Eligible employees can enroll in the company health plan on their first day of employment. Coverage options and employee contributions are described in the benefits enrollment guide.
