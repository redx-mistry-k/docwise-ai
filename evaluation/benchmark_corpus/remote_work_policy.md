# Remote Work Policy 2025

Employees become eligible for regular remote work after 90 days of employment. Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

The company reimburses up to $600 per calendar year for approved home-office equipment. Claims require receipts and must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.

Remote workers must use the corporate VPN and company-managed devices when accessing confidential information. This policy supersedes the 2022 remote-work memo.
