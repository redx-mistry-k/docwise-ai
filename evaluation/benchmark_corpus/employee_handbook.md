# Employee Handbook 2026

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year. Carried days expire on 31 March.

## Other Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick-leave days each year. Bereavement leave is 3 paid working days. New employees complete a 6-month probation period.

## Working Hours
The standard work week is 37.5 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.
